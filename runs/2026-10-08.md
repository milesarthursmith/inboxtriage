thread, where on 2 Oct Miles said "happy to pay today if discount is applied"
and raised a jammed roller shutter. He spoke last, so Roofrite owes him the

because a wrong archive costs attention and not data. It does not apply here:
an expired invitation is not retrievable by search — it has to be reissued by
someone else. That makes the cost asymmetric in the opposite direction from the
usual case, so both were left.
