## Tooling finding — `THREAD_VIEW_SUMMARY` silently drops threads

`search_threads` with `view: THREAD_VIEW_SUMMARY` returned **34** threads for `in:inbox`.
`THREAD_VIEW_IDS_ONLY` on the same query returned **40**. The 6 missing ones are the
unreadable threads below. `resultCountEstimate` read 40 in both cases and was, this time,
correct — but the SUMMARY/MINIMAL *thread list* was not.

**Count and enumerate the inbox with `THREAD_VIEW_IDS_ONLY`, never with SUMMARY or MINIMAL.**
A run that trusted SUMMARY would have silently never seen 6 inbox threads and would have
reported a wrong `inbox_count`. This is a sharper version of the 2026-08-29 note about
`resultCountEstimate`.
